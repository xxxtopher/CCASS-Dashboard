Daily CCASS change — all participants

Latest date in file. Click a column header to sort. Change = today − previous trading day.

Pick a From date above to see the change over a range instead: Change = holding at the To date − holding at the From date. Holders who exited by the To date are included, and holders who first appear in the range show as “new”. Clear the From date (or press “Latest day”) to go back to the latest daily change.
